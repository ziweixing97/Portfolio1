Recipe App Case Study

This recipe app is designed to help users learn how to cook and discover new recipes, making it a valuable tool for both beginners and experienced home cooks. It combines educational content with recipe exploration through step-by-step instructions, curated collections, and personalized recommendations. The app focuses on building culinary confidence by guiding users through techniques, offering inspiration from global cuisines, and making the cooking process more approachable and enjoyable.

Branding Design

I am redesigning the packaging for the tea brand TAZO. Due to growing concerns about the health effects of caffeine, TAZO has created a line of herbal teas that are completely caffeine-free.
